Questions

Good to know

Is Vifug Lyrics really free?
Yes - free to download, install and use on as many machines as your church needs. No licence fee, no per-seat cost, no watermark and no subscription. Anything you make with it - your songs, slides and recordings - is entirely yours, including for services you stream or charge admission to.
Can I modify it or build my own version?
Vifug Lyrics is free to use, but it is not open source - the software remains the property of its author, and copying, modifying or redistributing it needs written permission. That permission is often given freely for reasonable requests, so just ask.
Does it work without internet?
Completely. Songs, all seven Bible versions and your settings live in a local database on your machine. Church Wi-Fi going down will never take your lyrics down.
Which platforms are supported?
Windows (installer), macOS (DMG) and Linux (AppImage). Grab the latest from the download buttons above.
How do I set up AI auto-follow?
Auto-follow uses Deepgram for live speech recognition. Follow the Deepgram API key guide to get a free key, then paste it into Settings → AI auto-follow.
How do I get lyrics into OBS or NDI?
Enable native NDI in Settings → Streaming & output, or add the stream URL as a Browser source in OBS (1920×1080, transparent). Full walkthrough in the streaming chapter of the guide.
The stage display or phone remote will not load on my phone
Windows blocks incoming connections to new programs by default, silently - the page just times out. Open Settings → Streaming & output in the app and press Allow through the firewall. Full walkthrough in the guide chapter on letting phones connect.
Can I import my ProPresenter library?
Yes - .pro6 files import directly (plus best-effort .pro), and plain .txt / .docx lyrics are auto-detected into verses and choruses.
